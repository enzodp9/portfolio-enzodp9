## The problem

Bakeries and pastry shops manage production, recipes, costs, inventory, and
cash flow across scattered spreadsheets and systems that don't talk to each other.

## What I built

A SaaS platform that brings point of sale, production planning, recipes,
inventory, purchasing, cash management, and reporting together in one place.

## How

A product I designed, built, and operate end to end on Azure.
From zero to production; in real-world use since 2026.
